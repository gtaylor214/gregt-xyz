POLYRHYTHM

Sides are beats. A triangle strikes every 3, a square every 4, a pentagon every 5. Each keeps its own pulse, forever, and together they make a polyrhythm — the cycle is how many beats pass before the whole pattern comes back around.

Rings contract one per beat. Tap when a shape reaches the circle — the gold edge is perfect, for double damage.
Tap nothing on the empty beats. Shots arriving together clear with one tap.
Each hit damages the piece that threw it. Break them all and the rhythm is yours.
